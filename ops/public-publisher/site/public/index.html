How these indicators are measured

One source observation is assigned to its original minute. Cached responses never add successful minutes. Missing or failed observations stay visible. Daily planned reconnects count as downtime in the availability and relay indicators.

7 days is the primary SLO window; 24 hours provides early feedback. An assessment needs a complete window and at least 99% known observations. Conservative availability counts only confirmed good minutes over all expected minutes. A high observed value alone does not establish that the objective was met.

The daily renewal objective is 100%: the API must observe OFFLINE followed by LIVE with a different stream ID and a new start time, within 600 seconds of 00:00 JST. Unobserved earlier renewals remain unknown.

The APIs report provider state and relay counter progress. Playback, audio quality and visual correctness require separate receiver checks. YouTube retains its existing measurement definitions and history in its reliability tab.
